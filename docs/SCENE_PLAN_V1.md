# THANDU — Scene Plan V1

**Status:** Proposed creative storyboard, NOT approved for implementation.  
**Assets:** This is a public, non-sensitive scene framework. No real images, image numbers, dates, identities, private letter content, contact sheets or media paths belong in this document.

## Creative through-line

One warm guiding light travels through the whole experience and transforms its surroundings as the viewer discovers moments: **distant star → sanctuary glow → memory droplets → pathway lantern → flowering vine → festive garland → constellation → birthday universe**.

The app is a **guided, cinematic journey**, not a webpage sequence or slideshow. A separate **Memory Constellation** provides untimed, voluntary exploration after the finale.

**Initial narrative goal:** approximately 5–7 minutes, fully user-controlled, with skip/replay options.

## Motion and spatial language

- Each scene has an original *far environment*, atmospheric depth, midground structures, separate approved photo stage, foreground effects, and an unobtrusive accessible navigation layer.
- Touches produce immediate localized environmental reactions. **Scenery taps must never advance chapters unintentionally**.
- Transitions are motivated by the same travelling light. Avoid generic wipes, zooming card stacks, repetitive scroll panels and glitter overload.
- Celebrate contrast: opening is quiet, garden is alive, birthday courtyard is warm and playful, letter is contemplative, finale is grand.
- Reduce particle counts and parallax on slower devices; every important scene has a strong static fall-back.
- Protect natural photo aspect ratio; avoid facial crop, facial alteration, expression animation, generative reconstruction and effects over eyes/faces. See [Identity Preservation](IDENTITY_PRESERVATION.md).

## Interaction acceptance

Each guided chapter must have: a distinct, discoverable touch target; visible **Back** and **Continue** controls; audio status; pause/menu/skip access; keyboard equivalents; reduced-motion presentation; screen-reader alternative text for approved photos. A gesture is a reward, never an accessibility requirement.

## Look-development proof points

Before application build, produce and review:
1. SC-00 opening art and mobile entrance.
2. SC-02 *real, untouched landscape photo* placed in a beautiful wide memory stage.
3. SC-04 live touch-responsive garden flower/seed interaction.
4. SC-05 celebratory courtyard that feels premium, not crowded.
5. SC-06 readable letter chamber with motion off and sound off.
6. SC-07 world-merging finale rendered over time, not abrupt background swaps.

Review actual phone and desktop screenshots plus a short interaction test; do not approve from polished stills alone.
