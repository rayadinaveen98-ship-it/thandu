# Provisional Experience Structure (without personal assets)

**Status:** Draft storyboard architecture only. No photographs or personal captions included in this public document. Final photo-to-scene mapping remains private until approved.

## Creative through-line

**A life that brings light:** The experience changes from a quiet celestial threshold to discoveries of childhood, growing-up memories, and moments of joy, through a living garden and into a warm midnight letter. The birthday finale unites the worlds.

## Proposed chapters

1. **The Threshold** — touch a floating light; a gentle universe responds. A short title and intentional Enter control.
2. **First Spark** — early memories on glowing frames; small interactive lanterns reveal each moment; no invented dates or anecdotes.
3. **Growing Horizons** — spatial corridor of moments, school-era and later photographs only where context supports them; objects awaken environments.
4. **World in Bloom** — gardens emerge around approved expressive portrait photos; each touch blossoms a different part of the landscape.
5. **A Life Full of Light** — playfully joyful milestone memories, with special room for birthday celebrations; original video only if approved.
6. **The Midnight Letter** — the camera settles into a celestial observatory. Intentional opening of a sealed personal message.
7. **A Birthday Universe** — the atmosphere transforms from stillness to bloom to constellations; finale and revisit option.
8. **Memory Constellation** — optional non-linear gallery of all approved supporting photographs.

## Edit and direction controls

- A scene change must be user-intentional; tapping art is for interaction, not navigation.
- All original faces and facial appearance are fully preserved; see [Identity Preservation Rule](IDENTITY_PRESERVATION.md).
- Source history must be verifiable; no invented childhood moments, milestone dates or relationship labels.
- Write with an affectionate, warm, sisterly/familial rather than romantic tone.
- Individual editorial approvals needed for chosen photos, captions, music, letter, video use, and final publication path.
- Final quality goal is a living world, not a card slideshow. Use art direction, spatial transitions, and purposeful touch interactions.

## Review gate

After private photo audit, propose which images occupy each moment, then obtain owner's sign-off before app code or image processing.
