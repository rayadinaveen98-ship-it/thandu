## Purpose

Complete the six-chapter guided arc with a **visual reunion of the three previously established worlds**: celestial calm, blooming life, and joyful celebration. The same travelling light culminates in one deliberate, accessible interaction followed by a birthday message.

- Particle elements are independent and cleaned up on completion or scene exit; in-progress animation timers are cancelled on scene exit.
