## Approved-to-build vs proof-of-concept

At this stage, the five keyframe illustrations exist and a local/offline review HTML shows their overall look and simple independent touch particles / transitions. Neither physical 3D camera depth nor individual flower/architecture motion has been established from these paintings. Do **not** claim that these still images themselves are fully interactive worlds.

## Art production plan

1. **Choose look direction:** review all five keyframes against expectations; revise any that feel cluttered, unclear, or not distinctive enough.
2. **Layer breakdown:** far matte, atmospheric plane, independent architecture, midground, photo stage, foreground botanicals, glows and particles, navigation/accessible UI.
3. **Motion pilot:** produce *actual* touch-responsive moving lights in Entrance and flower-opening interaction in Garden, rather than faking motion by panning the whole keyframe.
4. **Faithful photo integration:** preserve original dimensions/aspect; never alter any face or artificially regenerate, retouch, warp, or animate facial features. Follow [Identity Preservation](IDENTITY_PRESERVATION.md).
5. **Performance and accessibility:** mobile-first 360–430px, progressive loading, 3D fallbacks, reduced-motion mode, visible alternative controls, opt-in music.
6. **Photo and publication approvals:** no private originals, video, contact sheets or personal manifest in public Git. A hosted deployment requires explicit image-release decision, separate from repo visibility.
