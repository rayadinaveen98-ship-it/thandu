# Photo Intake, Curation & Editorial Approval

**No photographs have been provided for this phase yet.**

## Photo intake workflow

The sender will share photographs privately in manageable batches, preferably by broad era: childhood, school years, early adulthood, recent/current, candid, favorites. Precise dates are optional and must **never be inferred** if unknown. No need for the sender to edit or rename everything in advance.

For each file, assess display resolution, aspect ratio, duplicate candidates, face-safe cropping, grouping context provided by the sender, whether others/minors appear, and storytelling potential. Prepare a contact sheet **outside public GitHub** and propose which image belongs in which chapter.

## Asset metadata (keep private)

```text
media_id
original_filename
confirmed_time_period (optional)
scene_candidate
crop_focal_point
other_people_or_minors_review
caption_source
display_approved
export_approved
optimized_derivative_location (local)
alt_text
```

Do not infer personal traits, exact ages, dates, places, or a life event from a photo. Do not make up memories. Keep original images unchanged and accessible only to their owner. Create optimized, metadata-stripped copies solely for approved distribution.

## Required approvals before app implementation / release

1. **Photo selection:** include/exclude, chronological truth, cover/hero frames.
2. **Creative storyboard:** opening, chapter order, visual techniques, transitions.
3. **Personal copy:** letter, captions, any teasing or sensitive references.
4. **Deployment:** public/unlisted/protected/offline, images safe for chosen access model.
5. **Final review:** real recipient experience on mobile and desktop.

Avoid unapproved jokes, identities, or sensitive details about the recipient or third parties. Do not commit images or contact sheets to the public repository.
